# Insight Health

> We build AI clinical agents that handle the patient-facing workflows clinical staff spend the most time on, including phone answering, fax processing, ambient note-taking, intake, follow-up, referral management, and triage.

Insight Health is built by practicing physicians for clinical practices that want to take administrative load off their staff without compromising patient safety, data security, or clinical quality. We are HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified. Our voice agent Lumi handled the first known autonomous clinical AI conversation with a patient in October 2023, and we have since handled more than 4 million patient conversations across 60+ medical specialties. Aura AI Scribe saves clinicians 2+ hours of charting per day, and our AI FrontDesk reduces average wait times by ~75% and call abandonment by ~60%. We integrate with the major outpatient EHRs via FHIR R4 and HL7v2, including exclusive AI provider partnerships with Office Practicum and Medicat that come with advanced integration not available to other AI vendors.

## Products

- [Aura AI Scribe](https://www.insighthealth.ai/ambient): Real-time, specialty-specific ambient clinical scribe that produces a SOAP-style note during the visit and saves clinicians 2+ hours of charting per day.
- [Aura Anywhere](https://www.insighthealth.ai/aura-anywhere): Embedded AI scribe SDK for EHR vendors and digital health platforms; lets a partner platform offer ambient documentation under their own brand and inside their own UX, with multi-tenant isolation and event-driven note delivery.
- [AI FrontDesk for clinic call centers](https://www.insighthealth.ai/ai-frontdesk-for-clinic-call-centers): 24/7 AI receptionist that answers patient calls, books appointments, and routes urgent issues, deployed in clinic front desks and centralized call centers.
- [AI Fax Agent](https://www.insighthealth.ai/fax-ai-agent): Inbound fax processing agent that classifies referrals, labs, prior auth requests, insurance and benefits, clinical records, prescriptions, medical-records requests, and EOBs, then routes structured data into the EHR in under 2 minutes with 99.5% extraction accuracy.
- [Pre-Visit Intake AI Agent](https://www.insighthealth.ai/pre-visit-intake): Voice or text agent that captures patient history and chief complaint before the visit so clinicians start the encounter with a populated chart.
- [Follow-up AI Agent](https://www.insighthealth.ai/followups): Automated post-visit check-ins that surface medication adherence issues, complications, and care-plan questions before they become readmissions.
- [Referral Management AI Agent](https://www.insighthealth.ai/referral-management): Inbound referral classification, scheduling, and follow-up so practices clear referral backlogs without manual triage.
- [Phone Triage AI Agent](https://www.insighthealth.ai/phone-triage): Symptom-based call triage that sorts urgent from routine and routes calls to the right clinical resource.
- [Direct Access Colonoscopy AI Agent](https://www.insighthealth.ai/direct-access-colonoscopy): Eligibility screening and scheduling agent for direct-access colonoscopy programs in GI practices.
- [Virtual Care Assistant](https://www.insighthealth.ai/solution-virtual-care-assistant): Enterprise virtual care assistant that bundles patient intake, scribing, and follow-up under one agent layer for larger practices and health systems.
- [Closing HEDIS gaps for health plans](https://www.insighthealth.ai/hedis): Voice and text agent for health plans that engages members directly to close HEDIS gaps across colorectal, breast, and cervical cancer screening, statin use and adherence, blood pressure control, and prenatal care.
- [Pricing](https://www.insighthealth.ai/pricing): Pricing for Aura AI Scribe (Free, Pro, Team, Enterprise) and outcome-based pricing for our AI agents (per call, per intake, per follow-up, per referral, per assessment).
